  • I'm not one for banners particularly, but I thought this one was particularly useful: sendit.com are a Video and DVD retailer who I have found to be absolutely wonderful when it comes to service. (They used to be called Blackstar... same people, same excellent service, just a different name.)
    My list of purchases from them is now quite extensive. They have set up 'Zones' for particular fan bases, such as the Farscape and Buffy Zones which keep you up to date on the upcoming releases --- if you order before release, you generally receive a discount. This is how I buy most of my Videos and DVDs.
    Enough advertising for now... suffice to say I've been impressed over and over by sendit.com, when there has been a problem, it has been quickly resolved by their excellent support team.
